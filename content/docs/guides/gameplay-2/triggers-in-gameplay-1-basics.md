# 1. What Triggers Can do in Gameplay

Every layout should start as a basic idea, but triggers can take it a step further. Instead of just a bland base that players can jump over, triggers can make the level feel more interactive, fun, enjoyable, and overall make it a more memorable experience. In this guide, we will explore the basics of triggers in gameplay and give you an idea of this unit.
